  - heading "B2B sellers who answer for their own pipeline" [level=2]
  - blockquote: "\"My CRM has the stage. It does not have why the deal actually slipped, and that is what gets asked.\""
  - paragraph: The company system records outcomes. Nobody records the evidence - the objection somebody raised in March, the proof you promised, the person who went quiet. So every review is rebuilt from memory and a scroll back through email.
  - list:
    - paragraph: Capture — Paste the thread. The account, amount, objection and next action come out and get filed against the deal.
    - paragraph: Today — Opens on the deals going silent and the promises you have not kept, ranked, with the reason attached.
    - paragraph: Review — Opens on what changed since the last review - what moved, what slipped, what went quiet - with every line traced to a record.
  - paragraph:
    - text: Walk into the forecast review with the answer already written.

    - text: You are also everything else
  - heading "Founder-led sellers, consultants and agency owners" [level=2]
  - blockquote: "\"Selling is a third of my week. The other two thirds is why I forget to follow up.\""
  - paragraph: There is no sales team, no ops person and no manager asking. Nothing goes wrong loudly - a proposal simply sits, a client stops replying, and by the time it surfaces the moment has passed.
  - list:
    - paragraph: Plan — One ledger of what is coming and what already happened, so the week has a shape you did not have to build.
    - paragraph: Proactive nudges — Capped at five. The few things that could embarrass you, not a second inbox.
    - paragraph: "Leads — The conversations that are not deals yet: who has never been contacted, who went quiet, who is ready to qualify, and who is parked until a date."
  - paragraph:
    - text: The follow-up happens on the weeks you are too busy to remember it.
  - paragraph:
    - text: The sale is only half the job
  - heading "Trading, distribution and supply" [level=2]
  - blockquote: "\"We won it in May, shipped in June, and I found out in August that nobody invoiced it.\""
  - paragraph: Winning is where most tools stop. The money is downstream of the win - a deposit, a delivery, an invoice, a payment - and every one of those steps is somewhere a deal can quietly stall with your cash inside it.
  - list:
    - paragraph: Orders — Contract to cash in seven steps, with how many days each order has been standing still.
    - paragraph: Cash Collection — Aging built from the payment terms already on the quote. Nothing re-entered.
    - paragraph: Cost Analysis — Goods, freight and duty landed against the sale - in its own currency if you buy and sell in different ones.
  - paragraph:
    - text: Know which order is stuck, on which step, holding how much.

  - heading "Long-cycle sales with procurement and a committee" [level=2]
  - blockquote: "\"The deal stalled at the last metre because nobody ever asked who signs.\""
  - paragraph: A long cycle is not one conversation, it is thirty across a year, spread over people whose roles you half-know. The gaps are invisible until the deal is at the line and one of them turns out to be the gap that mattered.
  - list:
    - paragraph: Stakeholders — Who you know, where your champions are, and which accounts have nobody.
    - paragraph: Business Vault — Ranks what you do not know by what the relationship is worth - starting with who signs.
    - paragraph: Search & Insights — Answers what happened with an account last quarter, computed on your device.
  - paragraph:
    - text: Find the missing decision-maker in month two, not month nine.
